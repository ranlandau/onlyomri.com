Frequently asked

FAQ

Is this related to any other website with a similar name?

Absolutely not. This is a fan club for one very specific colleague. Everyone stays fully clothed and fully caffeinated. The only thing exposed here is our admiration.

Will Omri bring hummus?

Yes. He will also have strong opinions about the hummus already in New York. Prepare accordingly.

Can I request a song?

Omri does not take requests. He does, however, take “quick questions,” which are never quick.

How long is the residency?

Long enough to learn the subway map, not long enough to stop complaining about it.

What should the NY office prepare?

A good chair, a working coffee machine, a whiteboard, and a healthy amount of patience for the phrase “in Tel Aviv we usually…”.

Who made this?

The Tel Aviv office, with love, mild jealousy, and far too much free time on a Thursday afternoon.
