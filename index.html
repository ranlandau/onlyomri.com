The Splitit years

A dramatic retelling of one man's career at a card-linked installments company, told with the seriousness it never asked for.

2017 · Tel Aviv

Integration Engineer

Joins Splitit in January. Plugs merchants into the platform, one API at a time. Learns that “the integration is done” and “the integration is live” are two very different sentences.

2018 · Tel Aviv

Onboarding Manager

Now he's also the one holding the merchant's hand through go-live. Develops the signature move: answering an email before the sender finishes typing it.

2019 · Tel Aviv

Pre-Sales & Implementation Engineer

Joins sales calls to explain how installments on an existing credit card work. Every prospect leaves the call understanding Splitit, and slightly wanting to be Omri's friend.

2020 · New York

Head of Implementation, US

Moves to New York for Splitit. The world locks down about a week later. He stays, sets up US implementation from a studio apartment, and learns every bagel place within a six-block radius. Wendy joins the tour.

2021–22 · NYC ↔ Atlanta

The Shuttle Years

Splitit moves its global base to Atlanta's “Transaction Alley”. Omri becomes a human ping-pong ball between LaGuardia and Hartsfield-Jackson, with a brief, humid Miami interlude. Delta considers naming a seat after him.

2022 · Tel Aviv

Head of Implementation, Global Operations · The Homecoming

Back in Israel, running implementation worldwide from the original room, which now has more than one WhatsApp group. Coffee machine finally fixed.

2026 · New York

The Comeback

Nandan says the words: make Splitit New York awesome again. Omri's hand is up before the sentence ends. Suitcase: already packed. Wendy: already at the door.

Frequently asked

FAQ

Is this related to any other website with a similar name?

Absolutely not. This is a fan club for one very specific colleague. Everyone stays fully clothed and fully caffeinated. The only thing exposed here is our admiration.

Will Omri bring baklava?

Yes. Not enough for everyone. It will be gone by 10:15. The Coffee Companion tier guarantees one piece; the Omri Special guarantees he'll tell you which bakery it came from and why it's better than the one you like.

Will Omri bring hummus?

He will bring opinions about hummus. He will have strong opinions about the hummus already in New York. Prepare accordingly.

Is Wendy coming?

Wendy is not coming with Omri. Omri is coming with Wendy. Know the difference. She has her own seat, her own paperwork and, we suspect, her own Slack account.

What does “Head of Implementation, Global Operations” actually mean?

It means when a merchant wants to offer installments on their customers' existing cards, Omri's team makes it go live. It also means he has said “it works in sandbox” on three continents.

Why does he keep flying to Atlanta?

Splitit's global base is in Atlanta, and the CEO is there. Also, at this point the hotel has his preferences saved and it would be rude to stop.

Can I request a song?

Omri does not take requests. He does, however, take “quick questions,” which are never quick.

What should the NY office prepare?

A good chair, a working coffee machine, a whiteboard, a water bowl at floor level, and a healthy amount of patience for the phrase “in Tel Aviv we usually…”.

Who made this?

The Tel Aviv office, with love, mild jealousy, and far too much free time on a Thursday afternoon. Splitit's actual marketing team was not consulted and would like that noted.
